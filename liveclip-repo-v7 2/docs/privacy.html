Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Short version: liveclipper runs on your own computer. The author does not receive, collect, store or sell any of your data.

What stays on your computer

  • Clips, thumbnails, titles, descriptions and settings are saved in folders on your computer.
  • If you connect a platform account, the login token and any keys you enter are saved in a file in your home folder (~/.config/liveclipper/), readable only by your user account. They are never sent to the author.
  • The control panel only listens on your own computer (127.0.0.1).

Who the tool talks to

Only the services you choose to use, directly from your computer: Twitch and YouTube to read streams and videos, and YouTube, Facebook, Instagram or TikTok to upload clips to your own account after you connect it and approve the upload. The tool may also load the YouTube video player in the panel when you preview a video. Those services handle your data under their own privacy policies.

What the author collects

Nothing. The tool has no analytics, no tracking, no accounts and no server run by the author. This website is a static page on GitHub Pages; GitHub may keep ordinary web server logs under its own policy.

Platform data

When you connect TikTok, Google/YouTube or Meta, the tool requests only the permissions needed to read your basic profile and upload videos you chose. It does not read other users' data. You can remove access at any time in each platform's security or connected-apps settings, and delete the saved token by deleting the file in ~/.config/liveclipper/.

Children

The tool is not directed at children under 13.
